This exercise gives sinusoidal quantities, not a circuit. Edit the values to explore their phasors and waveforms.

Formulas

ω = 2πf · T = 1/f
RMS = √(a² + b²) · φ = atan2(b, a)
Peak = √2 × RMS
x(t) = √2 × RMS × sin(ωt + φ)

RMS phasors and the sine convention used in the homework. The phasor rotates counterclockwise; its vertical projection, multiplied by √2, gives the instantaneous value. Drag the time slider to follow one period.
